# Outcome 0004 — Bound delegation three ways, not by depth

- Decision: 0004
- Status: observed
- Observed: 2026-09-13

## Expected

Each failure mode visible on its own, paid for with a test suite that must show
each bound doing work "rather than riding behind a stricter neighbour — every one
of the three cases satisfies the other two comfortably."

## Actual outcome

Held. `ai/jobs/lineage.py` enforces the three bounds at `TaskGraph.add()`, a
breach refuses the child and keeps the tree, and
`tests/unit/test_delegation_bounds.py` exercises each bound in a state that
satisfies the other two — which is the specific discipline the record asked for
and the one that is easiest to skip.

No breach has been observed in production use, because delegation is not yet
driven by anything but tests. So this outcome records that the mechanism is
correct and exercised, not that it has been stressed.

## Lessons

The record predicted the test-design cost accurately and in advance, and that
prediction is why the suite is not three tests that all pass for the same reason.
Naming the way a test suite will cheat, inside the decision, turned out to be
worth more than the decision itself.

What is not yet known is whether three bounds are the right three. That needs
delegation under real load, and there is none.
