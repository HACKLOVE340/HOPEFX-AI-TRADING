# Outcome 0009 — Ship the hand-landmark model, fetched at deploy time

- Decision: 0009
- Status: observed
- Observed: 2026-09-13

## Expected

The two `vision.*` rows go live and §4's derived presence roll-up with them —
**233/233**. The model becomes a deployment artifact fetched per environment, at
no cost to an operator who never enables camera gestures.

## Actual outcome

The number held and is still the measured one. `python scripts/backlog_report.py`
reports **233 of 233 spec capabilities live** on 2026-09-13, four days after the
record. `landmarkStatus` reports `unconfigured` when the model is absent and says
pointer gestures still work, so the deploy step is genuinely skippable rather
than nominally so.

The per-environment fetch is the live obligation this created, and it has not yet
been exercised against a second environment — there is one deployment path today.
So "must be fetched per environment" is a correct statement whose cost nobody has
paid yet.

## Lessons

The roll-up going from staged to 233/233 on the strength of one fetch script is a
reminder of how much a derived total can hinge on a single input. That is fine
when the derivation is measured — and `backlog_report.py` measures it from the
registries rather than being told — but a figure that moves that far, that fast,
is exactly the kind a document will still be quoting in six months. It is quoted
here with its date for that reason.

Reversing 0007 cost two days and produced a better answer than either record
alone. The pair is the argument for numbered, individually supersedable records
over a narrative.
