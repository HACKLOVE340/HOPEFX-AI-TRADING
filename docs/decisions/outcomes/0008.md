# Outcome 0008 — Operational intelligence stays in Group 1; Group 2 owns its inputs

- Decision: 0008
- Status: observed
- Observed: 2026-09-13

## Expected

One definition of inference and confidence. Cross-group references become
load-bearing, "so a broken one is a real defect rather than a broken link."

## Actual outcome

The single definition held — Group 2 references Group 1 rather than restating it,
and there is no second confidence model to drift against.

The predicted cost arrived and is larger than the record implies.
`scripts/docs_registry.py --check` measures **36 stale references** across the
document set today. The record correctly reclassified a broken cross-reference as
a defect; what it did not do was give that defect a gate, so the count is
reported and baselined rather than blocked. A reference that is "load-bearing"
and unenforced is load-bearing only in the sense that its failure is silent.

## Lessons

Declaring something a defect does not make it fail anything. That is Group 2
Rule 1 in miniature — *a new gate ships with injection evidence or is treated as
absent* — applied to a decision that created an obligation without creating the
check for it.

The reference count is the right KPI and it exists; what is missing is a ratchet
saying it may only shrink, in the shape `frontend_colour_ratchet.py` and
`model_provenance_report.py --check` already use. That is a small, known piece of
work, and it is the honest follow-up to this decision rather than a new one.
