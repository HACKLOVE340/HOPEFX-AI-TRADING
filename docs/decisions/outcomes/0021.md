# Outcome 0021 — The fiat wallet is the ledger the money path writes through

- Decision: 0021
- Status: pending
- Review by: 2026-12-17

## Expected

`wallet_transactions` starts being written, so AML's daily withdrawal **count**
and **volume** rules fire for the first time and `health_check_service.py`
aggregates something other than zero. Every money movement gains an
exact-`Decimal` audit row whose reconciliation check refuses rather than drifts.

The credit side must land before the debit side, because a debit against an
empty wallet refuses with "Wallet not found" or "Insufficient subscription
balance". The credit path needs a database uniqueness constraint, not a
read-then-write check, because Stripe delivers webhooks at least once and a
replayed `payment_intent.succeeded` that credits twice creates capital out of
nothing.

Money is *recorded*, not *disbursed*. `FIAT_PROVIDER` stays unconfigured.

## Actual outcome

`pending` — the decision is one increment old.

What has happened so far: the prerequisite is closed. `POST /payments/deposit`
now carries the authenticated `user.sub` into the PaymentIntent metadata, so a
confirmed payment is attributable to a wallet. `user` is a REQUIRED parameter of
`_fiat_deposit_impl`, not a defaulted one, because an optional identity is one a
caller omits in a hurry and the resulting intent is indistinguishable from a
correct one until the money arrives and cannot be credited to anybody.

Pinned by `tests/unit/test_a_deposit_is_attributable_to_a_user.py` — four tests,
all four red on the pre-fix tree, including one that deposits as two different
users and asserts they are not confused, because a module-level default would
have satisfied the single-user assertion and credited every deposit to one
wallet.

Nothing is measurable against the Expected section yet: no row has been written
to `wallet_transactions`, and the AML daily rules still have nothing to count.
That changes with Task 1 of the plan.

## Lessons

One, already, and it is the reason this record is not empty: **the obvious fix
was the wrong one, and it would have looked right.** `WALLET-DEAD`'s own
test-first note says to drive a withdrawal through the API and assert a row
lands in the ledger. Wiring `POST /payments/withdraw` through `debit_wallet` —
the one-line change the finding invites — would have given the register the
production caller it measures for, while making every withdrawal fail, writing
no row, and leaving AML's daily rules exactly as unfireable as before. The
finding would have read FIXED and nothing would have been.

It was caught by reading `_apply_movement` rather than the method name: it
auto-creates a wallet on credit and refuses on debit. That asymmetry is the
whole reason the task order is credit-then-debit.

Second, smaller: the identity gap was not in the register at all. It was found
while planning, by asking what the webhook would actually have to know. A
finding that names a fix does not always name its prerequisites.
