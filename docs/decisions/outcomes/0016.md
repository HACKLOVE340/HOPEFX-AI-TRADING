# Outcome 0016 — Root documents are authoritative; ARCHITECTURE is re-subjected

- Decision: 0016
- Status: observed
- Observed: 2026-09-13

## Expected

*"Takes the registry's contested-subject count from 3 to 0 and closes Group 3
item 8."* `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `DEPLOYMENT.md` at the root become authoritative
with the `docs/` copies marked `superseded`; `ARCHITECTURE.md` takes the subject
`architecture_module_map`, because it and `docs/architecture.md` are two
documents with two subjects, one of them mislabelled.

## Actual outcome

**The count is still 3.** Measured 2026-09-13 with `python scripts/docs_registry.py --check`:

```
note  [duplicate_subject] subject 'architecture'   claimed by ARCHITECTURE.md, docs/architecture.md
note  [duplicate_subject] subject 'contributing'   claimed by CONTRIBUTING.md, docs/CONTRIBUTING.md
note  [duplicate_subject] subject 'deployment'     claimed by DEPLOYMENT.md, docs/DEPLOYMENT.md
```

`docs/REGISTRY.toml:33` still reads `subject = "architecture"` for
`ARCHITECTURE.md`. The re-subjecting was decided and never written; the
supersede marks on the two `docs/` copies were never applied either.

The reasoning in the record is sound and nobody has disputed it. The edit simply
did not happen, and `--check` classifies the three as `note` / baselined, so
nothing failed and nothing said so. Four days of a registry reporting a state the
decision had already resolved.

## Lessons

The second of two decisions this ledger's first pass found **recorded and never
applied** (the other is 0014). Both were four days old. Neither was detectable
from the registry, which records what was decided.

What is specific here is that the decision's outcome was already a **number** —
"3 to 0" — computed by a script that runs in `pre-commit`. The gap between
intent and reality was one command away the whole time and nobody ran it,
because nothing connected the record to the check. That connection is exactly
what this ledger entry is, and it is the argument for `## Expected` being a
restatement someone can go and test rather than a paraphrase.

The wider lesson is about `note` as a severity. Three baselined notes are
indistinguishable from three notes that a decision promised to remove. A
baseline that a record has committed to clearing should ratchet — the pattern
`frontend_colour_ratchet.py` uses — or the promise has no mechanism behind it.

**Follow-up belonging to 0016:** apply the two edits the record specifies
(`ARCHITECTURE.md` → `architecture_module_map`; the two `docs/` copies →
`superseded`), then re-run `--check` and confirm 0. Until then this record
asserts a count the tree does not have.
