# Outcome 0006 — Treat 3D as projection, not WebGL, and make it earn its place

- Decision: 0006
- Status: pending
- Review by: 2026-12-13

## Expected

3D available on any browser, with `warrants3D` refusing more often than it
accepts — paid for by feeling obstructive "to somebody who just wants a pretty
chart".

## Actual outcome

The mechanism exists and is wired: `frontend/src/hub/surface3d.ts:151` exports
`warrants3D`, `VizMarks.tsx` imports it, and `surface3d.ts:360` gates rendering
on its verdict. Orthographic projection, with WebGL kept as an acceleration path
rather than a prerequisite, is what the module implements.

What cannot yet be observed is the half the record actually predicted — the
refusal *rate*, and whether it feels obstructive. `scripts/spatial_capabilities.py`
measures 16 spatial capabilities with 1 built, 9 partial and 6 planned, so the
surfaces that would exercise `warrants3D` in front of a user mostly do not exist
yet. Counting verdicts today would measure the test suite, not the product.

## Lessons

Provisional, and the reason this entry is `pending` rather than `observed`: a
decision whose consequence is a *rate* needs traffic, and recording "the function
is called" as its outcome would be the same measurement-that-cannot-fail the
ledger exists to prevent.

Review when more than one spatial capability is built.
