# Critical Design Flaws in HOPEFX-AI-TRADING

1. **Lack of Real-Time Data Processing**: The system does not have the capability to process market data in real-time, which is crucial for executing trades based on the latest market conditions.
2. **Inadequate Risk Management**: There are insufficient mechanisms in place to assess and manage the risks associated with trades, which can lead to significant losses.
3. **Poor Algorithm Robustness**: The current algorithms may not have undergone rigorous testing across varying market conditions, making them unreliable for real-world trading.
4. **Limited Scope of Trading Strategies**: The system primarily relies on a narrow set of trading strategies, which may not be effective in different market scenarios.
5. **Regulatory Compliance Issues**: There are potential compliance issues with trading regulations that have not been fully addressed, endangering the legality of the trading operations.
