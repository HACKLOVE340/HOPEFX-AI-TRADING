Getting Started
HOPEFX is an AI-powered trading platform specialising in XAUUSD (Gold/USD) and major forex pairs. It combines machine learning signal generation, real-time order flow analysis, macro data feeds, and automated risk management in a single platform.
Visit /register, enter your name, email, and a strong password. You will receive a verification email — click the link to activate your account, then log in at /login.

Trading & Signals
Signals are produced by an ensemble of models: LSTM for price sequence patterns, gradient boosting for feature-based regime detection, and a neural network trained on order flow imbalance. Signals are fused with macro data (COT, DXY, geopolitical risk) before being passed through a pre-trade risk gate.
Paper trading simulates real trades using live market prices without risking real money. It is the default mode for all new accounts. Switch to live trading only after connecting a broker and passing the risk assessment.
The kill switch immediately halts all new orders and optionally closes open positions. It can be triggered manually from the dashboard, via the API (POST /api/kill-switch/activate), or automatically by the risk engine when drawdown or loss limits are breached. It propagates across all server pods via Redis pub/sub.
OANDA (practice and live), Interactive Brokers (IBKR), and a built-in paper trading engine. Additional broker connectors (cTrader, MT4/MT5 bridge) are available on Elite plans.
