1. Who We Are

HOPEFX ("we", "us", "our") operates the HOPEFX AI Trading Platform, accessible at hopefx.io and via our API. We are the data controller for personal data processed through the platform.

For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@hopefx.io.

2. Data We Collect

Account data

  • Email address and username (required to create an account)
  • Password (stored as a bcrypt hash — we never store plaintext passwords)
  • Profile information you choose to provide (display name, country)
  • Two-factor authentication secrets (stored encrypted)

Trading data

  • Orders placed, positions opened and closed, trade history
  • Broker connection credentials you provide (stored encrypted at rest)
  • Backtesting configurations and results
  • Strategy settings and risk parameters

Usage data

  • IP address and user-agent string (for security and rate limiting)
  • Pages visited and features used (aggregated, not individually tracked)
  • API request logs (retained for 30 days for debugging and abuse prevention)
  • Error reports and crash diagnostics (via Sentry, if enabled)

KYC data (where required)

If your jurisdiction or subscription tier requires identity verification, we collect government-issued ID documents and proof of address. This data is processed by our KYC provider and is not stored on HOPEFX servers beyond what is legally required.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing the service: executing trades, generating signals, running backtests, and displaying your portfolio.
  • Account security: authenticating logins, detecting suspicious activity, enforcing rate limits.
  • Compliance: meeting legal obligations including AML/KYC requirements where applicable.
  • Platform improvement: aggregated, anonymised usage analytics to understand which features are used.
  • Communications: transactional emails (trade fills, risk alerts, password resets). We do not send marketing emails without explicit opt-in.
  • Support: responding to enquiries and resolving issues.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your trading data to train models that benefit other users without your consent.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract performance: processing necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: security monitoring, fraud prevention, platform analytics.
  • Legal obligation: AML/KYC compliance, tax reporting where required.
  • Consent: marketing communications (you can withdraw consent at any time).

5. Data Sharing

We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Brokers: order and account data is transmitted to your connected broker to execute trades. This is necessary to provide the service.
  • Infrastructure providers: hosting, database, and CDN providers who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements.
  • KYC providers: identity documents are shared with our KYC partner solely for verification purposes.
  • Error tracking: anonymised crash reports may be sent to Sentry if you have not opted out.
  • Legal requirements: we will disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for the lifetime of your account plus 90 days after deletion.
  • Trade history is retained for 7 years to meet financial record-keeping obligations.
  • API request logs are retained for 30 days.
  • Audit logs (privileged actions) are retained for 2 years.
  • KYC documents are retained for the period required by applicable law (typically 5 years).

7. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, bcrypt password hashing, JWT-based authentication with short-lived tokens, and role-based access controls. See our Security page for full details.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to security@hopefx.io.

8. Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • Session cookie: stores your authentication token. Required for the service to function. Expires when you log out or after 24 hours of inactivity.
  • Preference cookie: stores UI preferences (theme, layout). Expires after 1 year.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. You can clear cookies at any time via your browser settings; this will log you out.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Restriction: ask us to stop processing your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: for any processing based on consent (e.g. marketing emails).

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@hopefx.io. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

The HOPEFX platform is not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified by email and by a notice on the platform at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use of the platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

Privacy enquiries: privacy@hopefx.io

Data deletion requests: privacy@hopefx.io

We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 30 days.
