About HOPEFX

HOPEFX is an institutional-grade AI trading platform built for gold and forex markets. We combine machine learning, real-time market data, and rigorous risk management to give independent traders the same edge as professional trading desks.

Our Mission

Financial markets have historically been dominated by institutions with access to proprietary technology, data feeds, and quantitative research teams. HOPEFX exists to close that gap — delivering professional-grade AI trading infrastructure to individual traders and small funds at a fraction of the cost.

We believe that algorithmic trading should be transparent, auditable, and safe. Every signal the platform generates can be explained. Every risk parameter is configurable. Every trade is logged with full audit trail.

What We Build

AI Signal Engine

Multi-model ensemble combining LSTM, gradient boosting, and regime-detection classifiers. Signals are generated from live tick data, technical indicators, macro events, and order-flow analysis — then ranked by confidence and filtered through pre-trade risk gates before reaching the order router.

Risk Management

Real-time drawdown tracking, CVaR calculation, position sizing, and a hardware-level kill switch that propagates across all pods via Redis pub/sub. Prop-firm rule enforcement (FTMO, MyForexFunds, The5ers, TopStep) is built in.

Broker Connectivity

Native connectors for OANDA, Interactive Brokers, Alpaca, Binance, Bybit, and MetaTrader 5 via ZeroMQ bridge. A smart order router selects the best execution venue based on spread, latency, and fill quality.

Backtesting & Research

Tick-level backtesting engine with transaction cost modelling, walk-forward optimisation, Monte Carlo simulation, and multi-symbol portfolio analysis. Results are stored and versioned so strategy performance can be tracked over time.

Technology Stack

  • Backend: Python 3.11+, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, Redis, PostgreSQL
